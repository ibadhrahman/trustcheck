Responsible security

Honest limitations & security boundaries

We prioritize engineering honesty. Here is what TrustCheck does and does not do:

  • ⚠️
    Risk estimates only — not proof of payment: TrustCheck analyzes receipt integrity and transaction references. Only the seller can confirm final bank credit settlement before fulfillment.
  • 🔒
    Zero screenshot persistence: Uploaded payment images are analyzed entirely in volatile memory (RAM) and are never stored on disk or database, ensuring total privacy.
  • 🚫
    No QR codes: TrustCheck intentionally rejects spoofable QR codes in favor of unambiguous alphanumeric verification codes that humans can read and cross-check.
